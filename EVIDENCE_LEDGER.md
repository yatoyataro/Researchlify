## 1. Source Quality & Integrity Rules

- **Source types:** `primary`, `secondary`, `official`, `academic`, `user-provided`, `interview`, `survey`, `observation`, `other`.
- **Evidence strength:** `strong` (corroborated primary/official/empirical), `moderate` (single credible source or structured secondary), `weak` (anecdotal/uncorroborated), `assumption` (unverified hypothesis—must be labeled explicitly).
- **Mandatory integrity checks:**
  - Distinguish publication/collection date from access/retrieval date.
  - Never cite a source that was not actually read/consulted.
  - Never fabricate URLs, statistics, interviews, quotes, or survey counts.
  - Label unverified or missing-access claims clearly rather than smoothing them over.
